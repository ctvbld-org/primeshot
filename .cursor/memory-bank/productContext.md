# Product Context

## Problem Statement
Professional photoshoots are expensive and time-consuming, making them inaccessible for many individuals and businesses who need high-quality photos. Users need a way to create professional-looking photos without the overhead of traditional photoshoots.

## User Experience Goals
1. **Simplicity**: Guide users through a clear, step-by-step process
2. **Flexibility**: Offer various style options to suit different needs
3. **Quality**: Ensure high-quality output through input validation and AI processing
4. **Efficiency**: Minimize time from style selection to final photo delivery

## User Flow
1. **Authentication**
   - Email/password or social login
   - Profile completion required before proceeding

2. **Style Creation**
   - Select photography style (studio, natural, dramatic)
   - Choose outfit type (professional, casual, creative)
   - Pick background (plain, office, outdoor, custom)

3. **Payment**
   - Clear pricing display
   - Secure payment processing
   - Order summary and confirmation

4. **Photo Upload**
   - Drag-and-drop interface
   - Real-time quality validation
   - Progress tracking

5. **Review**
   - Quality assessment feedback
   - Option to re-upload if needed
   - Clear next steps

6. **Results**
   - Gallery view of generated photos
   - Download options
   - Share capabilities

## Target Users
1. **Professionals**
   - Need: Business headshots and profile photos
   - Priority: Quick turnaround and professional appearance

2. **Small Business Owners**
   - Need: Marketing and website photos
   - Priority: Cost-effective and versatile results

3. **Job Seekers**
   - Need: Professional profile photos
   - Priority: Affordable and professional quality

## Key Differentiators
1. **AI-Powered Enhancement**
   - Professional-quality results
   - Consistent style application
   - Rapid processing

2. **Guided Experience**
   - Step-by-step workflow
   - Clear instructions
   - Quality validation

3. **Accessibility**
   - Affordable pricing
   - Quick turnaround
   - No equipment needed
