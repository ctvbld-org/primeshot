# Split-screen reel

🎬 Split-screen reel: $9 vs $500 Comparison 

Goal:
Shock people with how good Primeshot’s AI photography looks compared to an expensive studio shoot.
You’re selling value + quality parity.

Duration:
7–12 seconds (ideal for TikTok + Reels reach).

Format:
Vertical 9:16 video (1080×1920).
Split-screen before/after or left = “Studio Shoot ($500)” / right = “Primeshot ($9)”

🧩 Step-by-Step Creation Guide
1. Collect Visuals

Left side (“$500 shoot”):

Use stock studio portraits: neutral gray background, controlled lighting.
Sources: Unsplash (“professional headshot”), Pexels.

Alternatively, create one in Primeshot using a “corporate headshot” style to mimic a real studio.

Right side (“$9 Primeshot”):

Generate 3–4 AI photos in Primeshot that look equally professional but slightly more creative or polished.

Style: “Contemporary Business” or “Editorial Neutral Light.”

Resolution: 2K or 4K for crispness.

2. Visual Layout (Split-Screen)

In CapCut or Canva Video:

Drop both photos side-by-side (1:1 ratio inside 9:16 canvas).

Add thin vertical divider line (white or brand color).

Label each side:

Left (top text): “Studio Shoot – $500”

Right (top text): “Primeshot – $9 / month”

💡 Keep labels visible the whole video — people scroll fast and need context instantly.

3. Add Transitions / Motion

Structure (~8–10 seconds total):

Time	Action
0 – 1 s	Text intro: “Can you tell which one cost $500?” fades in.
1 – 3 s	Split image slides in from opposite sides (or hard cut for punch).
3 – 6 s	Slight Ken Burns zoom-in on each face (gives life).
6 – 8 s	Quick cut to 2nd comparison pair (optional).
8 – 10 s	Text overlay: “Same quality. 50× cheaper.” + Primeshot logo + CTA (“Try it → primeshot.ai”).

Use fast beat-matched cuts — 1 per beat = engagement.

4. Add Music

Choose trending audios with clear transition beats:

TikTok search: “photo transition” or “comparison beat.”

Instagram Reels Music: “LoFi Focus” or “Cinematic Drop.”

Align your first switch (when the split appears) to the beat drop.

5. On-Screen Text

Use bold san-serif (like Montserrat Extra Bold / Poppins Black).
Add outline + drop shadow so it pops on any photo.

Text sequence suggestion:

“Can you tell which one cost $500?” → (1 s)

“Same quality.” → (5 s)

“50× cheaper.” → (7 s)

“primeshot.ai” → (8 s +)

Keep each phrase short — ideally ≤ 4 words per screen.

6. Finishing Touches

Add subtle brand watermark bottom-right (Primeshot logo at 30% opacity).

End card: “Your personal AI studio.”

Export 1080×1920 @ 30 fps.

7. Caption & Tags

Caption:

“Can you tell which one cost $500? 👀
Hint: The right side costs less than your lunch. #AIphotography #primeshot #headshots #founderlife”

CTA (in comments):

“Create your own $9 studio → primeshot.ai”
